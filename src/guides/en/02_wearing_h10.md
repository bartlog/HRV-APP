# Wearing the H10 Correctly

**Goal:** Stable ECG signal without movement artifacts.

---

## Step 1 — Prepare the Electrodes

- Moisten the two elongated **electrodes** on the inner side of the strap with **water**
- Alternative: electrode gel (ultrasound gel) for better contact
- **No alcohol** — damages the electrodes over time

---

## Step 2 — Position

- Place the strap directly **below the pectoral muscle**
- Sensor module: **center of chest**, slightly to the left (over the heart)
- Strap should be snug enough to move with your breathing, but **not slide around**

---

## Step 3 — Check Fit in the App

1. Open the app: `https://bartlog.github.io/HRV-APP/`
2. Open the **"Live"** tab → click **"Connect H10"** → connect to the H10
3. Watch the ECG curve at the bottom of the Live tab

R-peaks must be **clearly and evenly** visible.
If the curve is flat or heavily noisy → re-moisten electrodes, check fit.

---

## Step 4 — Movement Test

- 5 arm circles → ACC display shows deflections, ECG artifact indicator briefly appears (orange)
- Then rest: ECG normalizes within 5 heartbeats

---

## For Night Recordings (24h Offline Mode)

- Adjust the strap slightly **looser** (lying down: strap presses more)
- Moisten electrodes **generously** → lasts 6–8 hours
- Strap should not shift when turning over
  → Test: lie down and get up once, then check
